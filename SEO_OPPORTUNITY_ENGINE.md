# CHATR SEO Opportunity & Demand Engine

**Concept:** Systematic mechanism to identify, score, and prioritize search demand topics *before* creating content, connecting search intent directly to product capabilities.

---

## 1. Opportunity Scoring Model

Every potential commercial SEO topic is evaluated against a 6-factor commercial index:

$$\text{SEO Priority Score} = \text{Search Relevance} \times \text{Commercial Intent} \times \text{Product Fit} \times \text{Evidence Strength} \times \text{Freshness} \times \text{Conversion Potential}$$

| Dimension | Scale (1–5) | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search Relevance** | 1 to 5 | Does this query reflect how people actively search to solve real-world problems? |
| **Commercial Intent** | 1 to 5 | Is the searcher a business decision maker with purchasing budget (e.g. *"WATI pricing"*) vs generic curiosity? |
| **Product Fit** | 1 to 5 | Can CHATR solve this problem natively today without hypothetical roadmap features? |
| **Evidence Strength** | 1 to 5 | Can we demonstrate the solution with verifiable live software features and transparent pricing? |
| **Freshness** | 1 to 5 | Has there been a recent market catalyst (e.g. competitor pricing increase, Meta API fee changes, WhatsApp policy updates)? |
| **Conversion Potential** | 1 to 5 | Can the visitor experience the solution above the fold via an interactive demo before requiring login? |
