## I. Growth Metric Definitions (Single Source of Truth)

1. **Activated User**:
   - *Definition*: A registered user who has either (a) connected a WhatsApp Business API number, (b) screened their first candidate/lead, or (c) exported a customized resume/workflow.
   - *Why*: Pageviews, signups, and visitors are vanity metrics unless the user experiences the core utility.
2. **Qualified Visitor**:
   - *Definition*: A visitor who completes at least one full tool analysis or interacts with a shared candidate/customer scorecard.
3. **B2B2C Downstream User**:
   - *Definition*: A candidate or end-customer who was introduced to CHATR via a business's outbound workflow and subsequently created a free profile.
4. **Viral Coefficient ($K$-Factor)**:
   $$K = \left(\frac{\text{Total Invites Sent}}{\text{Active Base}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Invites Accepted}}{\text{Invites Sent}}\right)$$
   - *Target*: $K > 0.8$ across early cohorts.
5. **Day-7 Retention**:
   - *Definition*: Percentage of activated users who return to send/receive messages or screen candidates within 7 days of activation.

3. **Include the free utility tool as a helpful supplementary asset.**
