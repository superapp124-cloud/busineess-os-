# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

This directory contains records of all significant architectural decisions made during the evolution of the CHATR Intent OS platform. 

## What is an ADR?
An Architecture Decision Record is a short document capturing an important architectural decision made along with its context and consequences.

## Why use ADRs?
As the platform evolves and new contributors join, it is critical to understand *why* a decision was made, what alternatives were considered, and what trade-offs were accepted.
