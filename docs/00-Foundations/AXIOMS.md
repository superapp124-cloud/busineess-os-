## 1. The 6 Universal System Axioms

- **Axiom 1 (Dynamic System)**: Every organization or adaptive domain is a dynamic, interconnected state graph.
- **Axiom 2 (Observability)**: Every state transition is observable and emits immutable, verifiable empirical evidence.
- **Axiom 3 (Decision Transformation)**: Every choice or decision transforms the continuous-discrete state space of future trajectories.
- **Axiom 4 (Resource Conservation)**: Every execution consumes finite system resources (Time, Capital, Energy, Capacity).
- **Axiom 5 (Epistemic Uncertainty)**: Every prediction contains non-zero epistemic uncertainty ($\gamma \in [0.0, 1.0]$).
- **Axiom 6 (Historical Invariance)**: Learning updates future cognitive behavior without mutating historical transaction logs.
