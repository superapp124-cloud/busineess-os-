## 🧪 THE 3 MANDATORY PRODUCT TESTS BEFORE SHIPPING

Before shipping any feature, sprint, or component, it MUST pass all three tests:

1. ⏱️ **The 10-Second Test:** Can a new user understand why this feature exists in under 10 seconds? *If not, simplify.*
2. 💬 **The ChatGPT Test:** Would this still exist if ChatGPT were the primary interface? *If not, do not build a separate screen.*
3. 🌅 **The Monday Morning Test:** At 9:00 AM Monday, does this help someone decide what to do next? *If not, make it secondary.*
