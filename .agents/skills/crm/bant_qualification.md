# BANT Qualification Framework Skill

## Overview
Use this framework to evaluate lead qualification scores and compute deal health in Chatr CRM.

## Qualification Criteria

### 1. Budget
- **High (25 pts)**: Verified budget allocated > ₹500,000 / $10,000.
- **Medium (15 pts)**: Flexible budget allocated or discretionary spending available.
- **Low (5 pts)**: Undefined budget or exploring pricing options only.

### 2. Authority
- **C-Suite / VP (25 pts)**: Direct purchasing authority (CEO, CTO, VP Operations).
- **Director / Manager (15 pts)**: Key influencer with budget sign-off powers.
- **Individual Contributor (5 pts)**: End-user advocate without budget approval.

### 3. Need
- **Critical (25 pts)**: Active pain point impacting daily revenue or operational speed.
- **Moderate (15 pts)**: Desired optimization for existing workflows.
- **Low (5 pts)**: Nice-to-have upgrade with no immediate deadline.

### 4. Timeline
- **Immediate (<30 days) (25 pts)**: Urgency to close within the current month/quarter.
- **Short-Term (1-3 months) (15 pts)**: Planned deployment in upcoming quarter.
- **Long-Term (>3 months) (5 pts)**: Exploratory research phase.

## Score Classification
- **80-100**: Highly Qualified / Fast-Track Deal.
- **50-79**: Qualified Lead needing standard follow-up.
- **<50**: Unqualified / Nurture Campaign required.
