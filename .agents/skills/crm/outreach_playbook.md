# Outreach & Follow-Up Playbook Skill

## Overview
Guidelines for drafting high-converting, evidence-backed sales follow-ups and Next Best Actions in Chatr CRM.

## Core Rules

1. **Evidence Citation**: Reference recent news, tech stack details, or past meeting takeaways extracted in the lead dossier.
2. **Concise Value Prop**: Keep emails under 120 words. State clear business outcomes (e.g. 40% time saved in sales tracking).
3. **Single Clear Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Request a specific 15-minute meeting or approval step.

## Sequence Framework

- **Touchpoint 1 (Day 0)**: Value-first introduction referencing lead's company context.
- **Touchpoint 2 (Day 3)**: Case study or relevant ROI insight.
- **Touchpoint 3 (Day 7)**: Exec summary check-in with proposed discovery meeting times.
- **Touchpoint 4 (Day 14)**: Re-engagement nudge flagging unaddressed pain points.
